IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
---------------------
The main training corpora are broader spam/malicious-email datasets,
not purely phishing datasets. Therefore the primary supervised task
must be described precisely as malicious/spam email classification,
with phishing-focused external evaluation on Nazario.

No claim of general phishing detection performance should be made
without additional phishing-focused evaluation.

All dataset transformations and split decisions must be documented
to reduce leakage and support reproducibility.
